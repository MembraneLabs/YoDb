---
title: relations.yaml
description: Define approved relational joins and graph edges.
---

Relationships connect datasets, not arbitrary tables or columns. Every endpoint
field must already be mapped in `sources.yaml`.

```yaml
api_version: yodb/v0.1

relationships:
  customer_has_ticket:
    from: customer
    to: support_ticket
    description: A support ticket associated with a customer.
    cardinality: one_to_many
    direction: uni

    implementations:
      - from: {source: crm_postgres, field: crm_account_key}
        to: {source: support_postgres, field: customer_crm_key}

      - from: {source: relationship_graph, field: crm_account_key}
        to: {source: relationship_graph, field: id}
        edge_type: HAS_TICKET
```

Every implementation has `from` and `to` endpoints. Without `edge_type`, YoDb
compares the two mapped field values. With `edge_type`, it traverses that named
Neo4j edge between the two endpoints.

`direction: uni` means the relationship goes from `from` dataset to `to`
dataset. `direction: bi` permits traversal in either direction.
